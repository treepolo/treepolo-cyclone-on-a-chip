眼牆與風眼細化 / Eye refinement
在原本的區域裡，以區域中心的圓柱（區域跟著颱風走，圓柱就一直在颱風眼上）用較細的網格計算，水平與垂直都細化。細網格和外圍網格雙向耦合：圓柱外圍向外圍網格鬆弛，細網格每一步把平均回饋給外圍網格。/ A finer grid (across and up) in a cylinder at the centre of the running domain, which follows the storm, so the cylinder stays on the eye. Coupled both ways: around the cylinder the fine grid relaxes to the outer grid, and every step its means feed back into the outer grid.
