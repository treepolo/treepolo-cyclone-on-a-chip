區域非靜力模式 / Regional Non-hydrostatic Model

全可壓縮非靜力方程、笛卡兒 C 網格、RK3 + 聲波分裂步、六類冰相微物理（水氣、雲水、雨、雲冰、雪、霰）。雲（白：雲水＋雲冰）與降水粒子（藍：雨＋雪＋霰）以體積光線追蹤繪製，垂直比例放大。 / Fully compressible non-hydrostatic equations, Cartesian C-grid, RK3 with split acoustic steps, six-class ice microphysics (vapour, cloud water, rain, cloud ice, snow, graupel). Cloud (white: water + ice) and precipitation (blue: rain + snow + graupel) are volume ray-marched; vertical scale exaggerated.
