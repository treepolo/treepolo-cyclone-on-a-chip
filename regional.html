區域非靜力模式 / Regional Non-hydrostatic Model

全可壓縮非靜力方程、笛卡兒 C 網格、RK3 + 聲波分裂步、六類冰相微物理（水氣、雲水、雨、雲冰、雪、霰）。雲（白：雲水＋雲冰）與降水粒子（藍：雨＋雪＋霰）以體積光線追蹤繪製，垂直比例放大。 / Fully compressible non-hydrostatic equations, Cartesian C-grid, RK3 with split acoustic steps, six-class ice microphysics (vapour, cloud water, rain, cloud ice, snow, graupel). Cloud (white: water + ice) and precipitation (blue: rain + snow + graupel) are volume ray-marched; vertical scale exaggerated.

實驗 / Experiment

龍捲環境 / Tornado environment

改變環境（不改結果）：四分之一圓風徑圖的半徑與深度決定低層風切與 0–1 km 螺旋度；邊界層水氣越多，雲底越低、CAPE 越大。/ Change the environment, not the outcome: the quarter-circle hodograph's radius and depth set the low-level shear and 0–1 km helicity; a moister boundary layer lowers the cloud base and raises CAPE.
環境 / Environment—
舊版預設（WK82，10 m/s、2 km、14 g/kg）的 0–1 km 螺旋度只有約 120 m²/s²；現在預設 12 m/s、1 km、16 g/kg（約 280 m²/s²、CAPE 約 3200 J/kg、雲底約 0.9 km），是典型的強龍捲環境。/ The former default (WK82, 10 m/s over 2 km, 14 g/kg) has only about 120 m²/s² of 0–1 km helicity; the new default (12 m/s over 1 km, 16 g/kg: about 280 m²/s², CAPE about 3200 J/kg, cloud base about 0.9 km) is a typical significant-tornado environment.

颱風環境與參數 / Tropical-cyclone environment

只算半徑與高度（假設颱風左右對稱），同樣的方程與物理，快上百倍：先用它篩選參數，再到 3D 確認。改完按「套用並重新開始」。/ Radius and height only (the storm is assumed symmetric about its axis), same equations and physics, about a hundred times faster: screen parameters here, then confirm in 3-D. Press Apply to restart with the new values.
3D 颱風（15 km、5 km）使用同樣的海溫、濕度、輻射、最小風速與初始渦旋；改完按「套用並重新開始」。/ The 3-D tropical cyclones (15 km, 5 km) use the same SST, humidity, radiation, minimum wind and initial vortex; press Apply to restart.

先粗後細：用粗網格快速跑到有趣的階段，再按「細化」接著用細網格算（15 km → 5 km 颱風、2 km → 1 km 超大胞、1 km → 250 m 龍捲盒子，自動對準風暴）。/ Coarse to fine: spin up quickly on the coarse grid, then press Refine to continue on the finer grid (the 250 m tornado box is centred on the storm).

互動：只改條件 / Interaction: change the conditions

在「水平切面」或「合成圖」用滑鼠工具放暖泡、冷池，或塗海溫與陸地。這裡可以在模擬進行中突然改變環境的風切與中層濕度；之後怎麼發展完全由方程決定。模式沒有地形座標，所以不能放山。/ In the slice or composite view, use the mouse tool to place warm bubbles or cold pools, or paint sea temperature and land. Here you can change the environmental shear and mid-level humidity during a run; what happens next is up to the equations. The model has no terrain coordinate, so mountains are not possible.

挑戰任務 / Challenges

讓風暴自己達成目標：只能改條件（實驗、海溫、濕度、風切、暖泡、冷池、塗海溫或陸地），不能直接改結果。/ Get the storm to reach the goal by itself: change only the conditions (experiment, sea temperature, humidity, shear, bubbles, cold pools, painted sea or land), never the result.

無人值守實驗 / Unattended run

按一下就讓它自己跑：全速、快轉顯示，每隔固定模式時間記錄風暴統計，結束時自動存檔，報告存到雲端資料庫讓開發者直接讀取。/ Runs by itself at full speed, records the storm statistics at a fixed model-time interval, saves the final state and stores the report for the developer.
