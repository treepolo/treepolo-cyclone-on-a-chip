全球譜模式：靜力原始方程、σ 座標、半隱式時間積分。天氣系統只由物理方程演化，沒有任何「生成天氣」的程式。 / Global spectral model: hydrostatic primitive equations, σ coordinate, semi-implicit time stepping. Weather emerges only from the equations; nothing generates weather directly.

放大區域 / Zoom into a region

在地球上點一下選擇地點（黃框為區域範圍），再開啟區域非靜力模式：以目前的全球場為初始與側邊界條件（單向巢狀），解析對流、颮線與氣旋內部結構。 / Click the globe to choose a point (yellow box: the domain), then open the regional non-hydrostatic model there, initialised and driven at its boundaries by the current global fields (one-way nesting), to resolve convection, squall lines and storm structure.
所選地點 / Selected point—
嵌入：區域模式直接在地球上、照真實位置與比例跑，放大飛低即可看見雲塔；全球模式每小時提供新的邊界，並在領先超過 1 小時時等待。/ Embed: the regional model runs in place on the globe at true scale; zoom in and fly low to see the cloud towers. The global model refreshes its boundaries hourly and waits when more than an hour ahead.
